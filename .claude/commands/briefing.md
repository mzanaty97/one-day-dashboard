# Daily Briefing

Generate a smart daily briefing for Moustafa by doing the following:

1. Use the Gmail tool to fetch the 10 most recent emails from the inbox
2. Use the Google Calendar tool to fetch all events for today and the next 7 days
3. Analyse what you found and produce a structured briefing in this format:

---

## Good [morning/afternoon/evening], Moustafa 👋

**Today is [day, date].**

### 📅 Today's Schedule
List any events happening today. If none, say "No events today — a clear day."

### 📬 Emails That Need Attention
List the 3-5 most important or urgent emails, with a one-line summary of each. Skip newsletters and automated emails unless they are urgent.

### 🔮 Coming Up This Week
List any events or deadlines in the next 7 days worth knowing about.

### 💡 Suggested Priority
Based on the emails and calendar, suggest the one most important thing to focus on today.

---

Keep the tone friendly, clear, and concise. Do not list every single email — only what matters.
